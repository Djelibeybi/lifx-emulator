| T-03-05-01 | Denial of Service | lifecycle listener dispatch | medium | mitigate | Isolate/log each listener exception, use an immutable dispatch snapshot and preserve bounded async scheduling in the WebSocket bridge. |
| T-03-05-02 | Tampering | listener add/remove during dispatch | medium | mitigate | Identity-deduplicate registrations and apply registry mutations only to future dispatch snapshots. |
| T-03-05-03 | Repudiation | failed membership mutation | low | mitigate | Emit no event until repository/storage commit succeeds and retain serial-labelled diagnostics for callback errors. |

</threat_model>
