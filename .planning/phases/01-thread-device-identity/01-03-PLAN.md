<objective>
Complete the identity surface a Thread device presents: the `connectivity` argument reaches every
public factory, the WiFi radio reports no signal, and a matrix device's tiles report the same
firmware as their host instead of a hard-coded constant.

Purpose: CONN-01's full factory surface and CONN-03, plus the per-tile firmware mirror the SPEC
requires. All three are derived values that must fall out of the effective connectivity resolved
once in `DeviceBuilder.build()` step 1a — no handler learns that Thread exists.

Output: seven typed factories accepting `connectivity`, `wifi_signal` derived at build time, and
`StateDeviceChain` reporting host firmware per tile.
</objective>
